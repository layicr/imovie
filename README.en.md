# iMOVIE · Personal Movie Tracking Showcase

中文版：[README.md](./README.md)

iMOVIE is a personal movie & TV tracking showcase. With a Netflix-style streaming interface, it lets you elegantly browse and look back on your own "Plan to Watch / Watched" titles; whether on phone or desktop, you get a comfortable, immersive experience.

Live site: http://imovie.lyc.la
